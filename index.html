Learn Incident Response for Data Teams with Phoebe

A bad load, a leaked table, a wrong number on the board deck. Declare it, size it, contain it, tell the people who used it, and close it with evidence, on a live incident clock. The question underneath every session: when the data is wrong or out, how many people and decisions does it reach before you stop it, and could you show the board exactly what happened and when? Six leader sessions for the people who run the call, eight responder sessions for the people on call, one constructed company, Quarrybank, and its three incidents in one quarter.

Leader track 🧭 · six sessions, no code

For heads of data, analytics managers and the privacy or compliance partner who run the call: declare, set severity, staff the room, talk to executives and the board, and decide on a restatement.

🚨 Leader session 1 · start here

Is this an incident yet?

Quality, exposure, decision. A declare rule that fires on the first signal, and what waiting for a complaint costs: 14 + 7 against 10 + 4.

▶ Start here ⚖️ Leader session 2

Severity you can defend

Reach, sensitivity and reversibility in one matrix, so two leaders rate the same incident the same way.

 🧭 Leader session 3

Who runs the room

Commander, operations, comms, scribe. One owner, an explicit handover, and why rank stays outside the call.

 📣 Leader session 4

Telling people before they ask

Update cadence by severity, the holding statement, the executive update and the partner note.

 📉 Leader session 5

When the board already used the number

Decision incidents: which decisions used the wrong figure, restate or footnote, and materiality as a disagreement.

 🗓️ Leader session 6

Ready before the next one

The tabletop, three readiness numbers and the one-page plan, signed.

Responder track 🔧 · eight sessions, one incident clock

For data and analytics engineers on call: keep the timeline, trace what read the bad data, contain, correct and notify. Session 8 replays the whole quarter.

⏱️ Responder session 1 · start here

The timeline starts at the first signal

The living record, six fields per row, every time in UTC, and why you never drop the bad table.

▶ Start here 🔎 Responder session 2

What read the bad data

A consumer trace from the bad write: tables, dashboards, extracts, emails and models, growing every hour.

 🛑 Responder session 3

Stop a bad load spreading

Pause, quarantine, break the circuit, and contain before you fix.

 🚪 Responder session 4

Close a leaked table's doors

Revoke every link and share, rotate, snapshot first, and find what was already copied.

 ✅ Responder session 5

Correct, then prove it landed

Fix forward or roll back, then check every consumer reads the corrected rows.

 ✉️ Responder session 6

Tell every consumer who read it

The notice list generated from the trace, not from whoever complained.

 🗂️ Responder session 7

Close with evidence

Exit criteria, the record the postmortem needs, follow-ups with owners.

 🕰️ Responder session 8

The incident clock

The whole quarter replayed under six plans and three traps, and the plan scored. 14 + 7 to 5 + 1.
